# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI4IA serves enterprise knowledge workers who need grounded multimodal assistance,
agent builders who configure governed personas and tools, and administrators who
operate the service. They work in long-running conversations where model choice,
documents, memory, tools, voice, usage, and governance must remain understandable
without taking attention away from the task.

## Product Purpose

AI4IA is a governed multimodal agent-chat workspace. It combines durable
conversations, catalog-driven models, agents, authorized tools, document context,
personal memory, voice, usage insight, and operational telemetry while keeping the
API authoritative for identity, ownership, feature gates, routing, and execution
safety. Success means users can move between typed, document, tool, and voice work
without losing context or wondering which settings or instructions are in effect.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, capable, calm, and direct. The interface should feel operationally
serious without becoming intimidating: familiar controls, plain language, visible
governance, and quiet confidence rather than decoration.

## Anti-references

- Crowded toolbars and composers that compete with the conversation.
- Duplicate controls or conflicting instruction sources.
- Nested cards, ornamental dashboards, and unexplained metrics.
- Siloed studios for capabilities that belong in the conversation.
- Hidden permissions, approvals, ownership, risk, or data-freshness states.
- Novel interaction patterns where standard product affordances are clearer.

## Design Principles

1. **One effective source of truth.** Show the server-owned model, instructions,
   agent, tools, and context that will actually run.
2. **Conversation first.** Preserve maximum useful space for the transcript and
   composer; reveal configuration progressively in a contextual inspector.
3. **Governance is part of the workflow.** Explain inheritance, risk, approval,
   scope, ownership, freshness, and unknown states at the point of use.
4. **Capabilities stay connected.** Documents, media, tools, memory, usage, and
   voice should augment the active conversation rather than become separate worlds.
5. **Familiarity earns trust.** Prefer consistent, standard controls and concise
   language over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA across light and dark themes and retain the high-contrast theme.
Every workflow must support keyboard and screen-reader use, visible focus, 200%
zoom, reduced motion, non-color state cues, and adequate text and placeholder
contrast. Drawers, tooltips, menus, and popovers must manage focus and remain
unclipped on narrow screens.
